### R2-1 — Medium: approach point equal to a base node

Evidence: design lines 290–311; plan lines 223–228. A rectangle ends at x=1, its outward port is `(1,0)`,
clearance is zero and approach margin is 0.125. The first valid dyadic approach is `(1.125,0)`. If a base node
is there, it is the nearest candidate, producing a zero-length outer connector. Revision 2 distinguishes the
port and approach, but leaves this different equality case unspecified. Rejecting the connector would lose a
valid connection; creating two co-located traffic nodes would invent a reservation boundary.

**Disposition:** accepted. Revision 3 aliases the approach address to the selected base node and omits the outer
edge. The positive-length port-to-base edge keeps connector attributes. Aliases share the base node's capacity
and traffic resource; alias-targeted overrides use normal ordering. An exact override of the absent outer edge
is stale. Task 6 adds automatic/explicit target, direction, required-path and capacity tests.

### R2-2 — Medium: explicit graph snapshot cannot recover discarded input

Evidence: design lines 323–325; plan lines 193–208; `intralogistics/graph.py` lines 76–82. Current LayoutGraph
deduplicates identical Nodes and overwrites repeated directed edges during construction. The reviewer reproduced
`LayoutGraph([a,a,b], [Arc(a,b,False,1), Arc(a,b,False,2)])`: only two nodes and an effective speed-2 edge remain.
Therefore a subsequent `Explicit(graph)` cannot validate duplicates that no longer exist in observable data.

**Disposition:** accepted. Revision 3 snapshots and validates the observable nodes/effective directed adjacency.
No promise to recover discarded history, and no change to no-layout graph construction. Raw JSON explicit
definitions are checked before compilation, where duplicate information exists. Task 5 tests the distinction,
including overlapping bidirectional/directed definitions and caller-graph preservation.

### R2-3 — Medium: public mutation after binding

Evidence: design lines 537–549, 564–574, 585–603; plan lines 248–260; existing `AGV.current_node` setter at
`agv.py:160`, warehouse bay lists at `warehouse.py:56`, charging/parking node attributes at `charging.py:74`
and `parking.py:51`. Constructor-only checks do not constrain later mutations. A foreign Node can enter AGV
state; an equal copy takes the setter's equality fast path and replaces the canonical object; appending a bay
can change a finalized mapping without updating its saved identity.

**Disposition:** accepted. Revision 3 makes layout-mode static endpoint/graph bindings read-only after bind.
Warehouse bays are tuples; station/parking nodes and component graph properties reject replacement. AGV
movement accepts only exact enabled graph nodes or None, checked before mutation/equality shortcuts. Internal
relocation obeys the same invariant. Existing no-layout setters/lists remain unchanged. Task 7 adds these cases.
This boundary is explicitly proposed as A5, not silently inserted into approved global contracts.
